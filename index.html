07 / About Mohamed

One Career.
Multiple Dimensions.

Mohamed Falfoul is a medical resident in Child & Adolescent Psychiatry whose professional journey also spans strategic consulting, organizational development, capacity building and youth advocacy.

His work bridges clinical experience and institutional thinking, connecting scientific knowledge with policy, program design and participatory leadership.

From hospital-based education to youth-centered international forums, his engagements focus on strengthening people, organizations and systems.
